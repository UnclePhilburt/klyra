Community

Help test the front.

KLYRA is in pre-alpha. The useful thing you can do right now is play it and say what broke.

01

Play a match

Download the desktop build, pick a faction, and stay in long enough to see the zone move.

02

Write down the break

What you were doing, what you expected, and what the game did instead. A screenshot helps.

03

Bring a second player

Online rooms hold up to 12 people. On one computer, a second controller can join the same fight.

Discord

Reports, ideas, and groups.

Bug reports, suggestions, and multiplayer groups belong on the KLYRA Discord. The invite is not posted on this page yet. When it is ready, the join button goes here.

Until then, play from the download page. Do not send reports to a server that is not this game.
