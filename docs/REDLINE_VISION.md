# KLYRA: Redline vision

Source: Cody's Redline vision, 22 Oct 2026. Structured from the key content passed on; replace with the full original text if needed.

Redline is a KLYRA racing game in two disciplines: **Redline Pro** (professional circuit racing) and **Redline Street** (underground street racing). It is in development. The roadmap is development order only, with **no dates**.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Vehicle Foundation
Acceleration and braking; automatic and manual transmissions; engine RPM simulation; realistic engine audio; gear shifting; suspension and handling; vehicle cameras; speedometer and tachometer; keyboard and controller support.

### Phase 2: Professional Racing Circuits
Circuits; elevation changes; banked turns; bridges and track crossings; pit lanes; starting grids; checkpoints; lap timing; spectator areas. Some circuits may have dramatic jumps and unconventional layouts.

### Phase 3: Multiplayer Racing
Dedicated racing servers; lobbies; matchmaking; countdowns; vehicle sync; race results; finishing positions; friends and parties; returning to the KLYRA Social Hub.

### Phase 4: AI Racing
Racing lines; cornering and braking; overtaking; collision avoidance; adjustable difficulty; filling empty positions.

### Phase 5: Initial Redline Pro Release (targets, not guaranteed)
2 circuits; 4 vehicles; 8 racers including bots; Quick Race; Time Trial; Multiplayer Race; race HUD and results; basic leaderboards.

### Phase 6: Pro Expansion (potential)
More vehicles and circuits; qualifying; championships; driver rankings; tuning; liveries; pit stops; tire wear; spectator systems; replays.

### Phase 7: Redline Street
Urban environments; night racing; highway races; sprint races; drifting; customization; performance upgrades; street reputation; multiplayer street racing; AI opponents. Its own visual identity and progression, sharing technology with Pro.

### Phase 8: Future Expansion (ideas only)
Rally; off-road; drag; endurance; stunt; demolition; community-created circuits.
