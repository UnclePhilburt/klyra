Development

Built by one developer.

KLYRA: Broken Front is an independently developed military sandbox created by a solo developer with a passion for large-scale warfare and player-driven gameplay.

From infantry combat and helicopters to logistics, weapon customization, and forward operating bases, KLYRA is being built and expanded one system at a time.

Currently in pre-alpha, the game continues to evolve through development, testing, and community feedback.

The goal is to create a battlefield where every player has a role and every battle tells a different story.

October 11, 2026

The pre-alpha is playable.

The desktop build has the three factions, a moving front that ends at 100 points, helicopters, supply trucks, forward bases, a weapon builder, revive, and a stash for gear you extract. Online rooms hold up to 20 players. The field fills toward 60 soldiers, 20 on a side. Two people can also play on one computer with two controllers.

In the workshop

The battlefield is getting quieter.

Weather around the fight is being turned down so guns, trucks, and helicopters stay in front of the wind and rain. Bird and animal sounds are being taken off the test map. A warzone should not sound like a nature reserve.

That pass is in the project now. It is not in the desktop download yet. It ships when the next build goes out.
