The battlefield

What you can do.

Large-scale fights, a job for every player, and a front that does not sit still. These clips and frames are from the pre-alpha.

Play

Pre-alpha version

01

Three factions

Ironclad, Timberwolf, and Vanguard share one battlefield. The zone moves. First side to 100 points wins.

02

60 bots

The match fills out to 60 soldiers, 20 on a side. A player on that side takes one of those slots.

03

Multiplayer

Online rooms hold up to 20 players. Everyone else on the field is part of that soldier count.

04

Split-screen

Two players, one computer. Both people play with a controller.

05

Helicopters

Get in a helicopter, fly the fight, and move people across the front.

06

FOB building

Put up a forward operating base and keep it in the fight with supplies.

07

Logistics

Trucks carry soldiers and supplies. The front only holds if someone runs them.

08

Weapon builder

Build a weapon from parts and take that loadout onto the field. Gear you extract stays in your stash.
