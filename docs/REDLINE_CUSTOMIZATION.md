# Redline garage customization (preview)

Web preview of planned in-game part customization in the Redline garage viewer (`/games/redline/garage/`). Source: Synty POLYGON Pro Racer and Street Racer part FBXs from the Unity project (read/copy only), converted with `cz.py` in Blender 4.2 into one Draco + WebP GLB per car, normalized exactly like the stock garage GLBs so parts line up.

## How it works

- Stock garage loads `/cars/<id>.glb` as before. Parts are lazy-loaded only when the user presses **Customize parts**: `/cars/custom/<id>.json` (slot list) and `/cars/custom/<id>.glb` (base + every variant), plus per-variant thumbnails in `/cars/custom/th/<id>/`.
- GLB node names: `V_<Slot>_<NN>` (part variant), `Wheel_<FL|FR|RL|RR>_Stock` and `W_Wheel_<pos>_<NN>` (wheel styles). Bonnet/Boot variants sit under the `Hood`/`Trunk` hinge so open-hood still works.
- Slots that have a factory default show only real variants; slots without one (Fenders, Rollcage, Spoiler on some cars, stickers…) add a **None** option.
- Wheel styles use the Pro Racer `SM_Veh_Wheel_Wide_01–07` set, scaled to each car's stock wheel. Not offered on kart, open-wheeler, vintage, race truck or motorbike (their wheels are bespoke).
- Paint schemes are the real alt materials from the Synty packs in the Unity project (`Assets/Synty/PolygonProRacer/Materials/Alts`, `Assets/Synty/PolygonStreetRacer/Materials/Alts` and `Materials/Cars`), read-only. Each is a full atlas swap of the `paint` material (`/cars/paint/<id>.webp`, 2048px, lazy-loaded; thumbs in `/cars/paint/th/`; list in `/cars/paint/schemes.json`, generated by `/workspace/cars/paintgen.py`).
  - Pro (24): 18 liveries mapped by texture GUID (Chug, Syncorp 01/02, Corsa, Syncola 01/02, High Voltage, Sike, Star Power, A1 Skidmark, Global Edge, Dragon Fire, Anime Punch, Big Cat, Royal Gas, Cyber Blade, Carbon, Checkered) and 6 solids (Black, Blue, Green, Red, White, Yellow) baked from `PolygonProRacer_Vehicle_19` + `Colour_Change_Mask_01` with each material's `_Skin_Colour`.
  - Street (55): the 12 atlas alts `PolygonStreetRacer_01_A`–`04_C`, plus 43 `Veh_*` vinyl/pearl/stripe/rust materials. Street truck adds 5 `Truck_*` vinyls, motorbike adds 2 `Bike_*` police vinyls.
  - `?paint=<scheme id>`; old hex links (`paint=18c8ff`) or a scheme from another pack fall back to factory paint. Rims still tint `rim`.
- Every part slot has a remove option: **None** when the slot has a factory part (removing it leaves a gap), **Stock** when it is an add-on slot (removing it gives the bare stock car). Encoded as `<Slot>.none`. Wheels cannot be removed.
- Shareable URL: `?car=<id>&parts=<Slot>.<NN|none>-…-Wheels.<NN|stock>&paint=<hex>`.
- Dirt bike has no part FBXs and stays non-customizable.
- Excluded from conversion (not body parts / need rigs): netting, screen banners, trailer axle/hitch, seats, truck stock wheels, door-specific fender variants, flatbeds.

## Hotspot UI

- After **Customize parts**, each slot gets a small cyan hotspot (44px tap target) anchored on the part: top surface of solid parts (raycast down), or the camera-facing side for split parts (skirts, fenders); Wheels anchor on the front wheel facing the camera.
- Hotspots are projected every frame, so they follow the orbit; a throttled (120 ms) raycast hides ones behind the body (glass ignored), and overlapping ones are decluttered (38px).
- Tapping opens a pop-up next to the hotspot with thumbnails and the current choice; at stage widths under 600px it docks as a compact bottom sheet over the lower part of the stage. Esc / outside tap closes it.
- Stage bar: preview label, paint swatch button (paint pop-up), Reset, Share build (copies URL). Rims stay in Controls.

`/workspace/cars/czall.sh` (cz.py per car) then `czthumb.py` for thumbnails; copy `out/cz_<id>.{glb,json}` to `public/cars/custom/`.

## Test drive (website mini preview)

- **Test drive** button on the garage stage opens a fullscreen overlay (`app/games/redline/RedlineDrive.jsx`, code-split, loaded only on click). Exit / Esc returns to the garage with the build untouched; the garage render loop pauses while driving.
- Uses the visitor's car: same GLB (custom GLB with the build when the customizer is open, else stock), parts, wheels and paint scheme.
- Track: procedural ~1.1 km closed Catmull-Rom loop (asphalt, red/white kerbs, run-off) dressed with instanced Synty props from `/cars/track/kit.glb` (480 KB, Draco + WebP; built by `/workspace/cars/trackkit.py` from FBXs in `/workspace/cars-src/road`): concrete striped barriers (straights), tyre barriers (bends), tyre stacks, cones, starting line and grid, gantry, grandstands, pit wall, lights, trees.
- Arcade physics (custom, 120 Hz fixed step): throttle/brake/reverse, speed-sensitive steering, lateral grip decay (handbrake = drift), off-track slowdown, wall push-back. Lap = cross start line after two checkpoints; best lap in `localStorage["klyra.redline.testdrive.best"]`.
- Controls: WASD/arrows, Space handbrake, R reset, Esc exit; touch: ◀ ▶ bottom-left, Gas/Brake bottom-right.
- Perf: no shadow maps (blob shadow), pixel ratio cap 1.25 mobile / 1.75 desktop, no AA on mobile, fog, rAF stops when the tab is hidden.
