The war

Fight, haul, and keep it.

Someone has to take the ground. Someone has to drive. A base with nothing in it cannot hold.

01

Fight

Take the hot zone with your team. Loot weapons off the field. Pick up the soldier next to you before the fight moves on.

02

Haul

Trucks and helicopters move people and supplies. If the crates do not arrive, the base goes quiet.

03

Keep it

Build a forward base and stock it. Gear you get back to safety goes into your stash for the next fight.

The front does not sit still.

01

Drop in

You join red, green, or blue. The first team to 100 points wins.

02

Move

The battle zone shifts. Stay on it, or go run the trucks, the helicopters, and the supplies.

03

Get out

A teammate can bring you back. If you do not make it out, the gear you were carrying is still out there.
